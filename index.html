About the Author

M.L. Goodlow

Goalie. Author. Advocate.

M.L. Goodlow is a new author based in the Pacific Northwest, where she lives alongside her battle buddy and loyal service dog, Seti. When she's not writing, she can often be found on the ice as a goalie in Para Ice Hockey, getting lost in a good book, or planning her next travel adventure. A passionate hockey fan, she brings the same dedication, focus, and heart to the sport that she does to her storytelling.

Rain, evergreens, and hockey all somehow make perfect sense together in the Pacific Northwest she calls home. A proud Air Force veteran of 16 years and a retired state trooper after 25 years of service, M.L. traded in her uniforms for storytelling — but still brings the same dedication and grit to everything she does.

She's just beginning her writing journey, bringing a lifelong love of storytelling and found family to the page. "I've always had stories in my head," she says, "but one day I decided it was time to share them instead of keeping them to myself." She writes M/M sports and contemporary romance because she loves creating characters who find love, hope, and happiness despite life's challenges.

Behind the Blue Lines: My Journey to Becoming an Author

By M. L. Goodlow

If someone had told me a few years ago that I would become a published author, I probably would have laughed. Yet here I am, having turned something that started as an idea into my very first novel, Between the Blue Lines.

I’ve always loved reading, especially M/M romance. There’s something special about getting lost in a story, connecting with the characters, and experiencing all the emotions that come with their journey. But being a reader and actually writing a book are two completely different things!

Becoming an author wasn’t something I had originally planned, but sometimes life takes you in directions you never expected. This has certainly been one of those adventures.

Where It All Began

My love for hockey played a big part in bringing Between the Blue Lines to life. I’m a huge Seattle Kraken fan, and I also play para ice hockey as a goalie for the Seattle Kraken Para Ice Hockey Team. Hockey is a big part of my life, both on and off the ice.

So, when I decided to write my first book, it only made sense that hockey would be at the center of it.

I wanted to combine two things I enjoy: hockey and M/M romance. But I didn’t want to write just another hockey love story. I wanted something with heart, emotion, challenges, and characters readers could connect with.

That’s how Austin Axman and Tristan Reece came into my life.

Bringing Austin and Tristan to Life

Between the Blue Lines follows Austin Axman, the confident and caring captain of the Forest River Evergreens, and Tristan Reece, a talented but guarded goaltender.

Both men have their own struggles, fears, and personal challenges. As their friendship develops into something more, they must learn to trust each other, open their hearts, and face the pressures that come with being professional athletes.

I wanted their story to be about more than two men falling in love. I wanted to explore the importance of mental health, friendship, acceptance, trust, and having people in your corner when life throws you a curveball.

One of my favorite parts of writing this book was creating the Forest River Evergreens. I didn’t want Austin and Tristan to exist in their own little world. I wanted teammates who joked around, supported each other, gave each other a hard time, and became a family both on and off the ice.

Being part of a hockey team myself, I know how important teammates can become. You spend time together, work through challenges, celebrate victories, and support one another through difficult moments. That sense of camaraderie was something I wanted readers to experience.

And believe me, some of those Evergreens players have personalities of their own!

The Challenges of Writing My First Book

Writing a book sounds easy until you actually sit down and try to do it!

There were days when the words flowed, and I couldn’t type fast enough. Then there were days when I would stare at the screen, wondering what in the world I was doing.

I quickly learned that writing isn’t just about putting words on paper. It’s about developing characters, building relationships, keeping the story moving, and making sure everything comes together.

There were plenty of moments when I questioned myself. Was the story good enough? Would readers connect with the characters? Would anyone even want to read something I wrote?

But I kept going.

I also discovered just how much goes into publishing a book beyond writing the story. Editing, formatting, cover design, and figuring out the publishing process were all new experiences for me.

There were definitely moments of frustration, along with plenty of learning experiences. But each challenge taught me something new.

If there’s one thing I’ve learned throughout my life, it’s that you don’t give up just because something becomes difficult.

And writing my first book was certainly no exception!

More Than Just a Hockey Romance

One thing that was especially important to me while writing Between the Blue Lines was showing that even strong, successful people can struggle.

Professional athletes may appear confident and fearless on the outside, but that doesn’t mean they don’t experience fear, insecurity, or emotional challenges.

I wanted Austin and Tristan to feel like real people. They make mistakes, have difficult conversations, and sometimes struggle to figure out what they want or need.

Mental health is an important part of their story. I wanted to show that experiencing emotional challenges doesn’t make someone less capable, less deserving of love, or any less of a person.

Their journey is about love, but it’s also about finding the courage to be yourself and understanding that asking for support doesn’t make you weak.

Sometimes the hardest thing a person can do is allow someone else to see who they truly are.

I also wanted to explore the challenges that can come with being in a same-sex relationship in professional sports. The fear of judgment, the pressure of public attention, and the uncertainty of how others might react are all things that can affect someone’s willingness to be open about who they love.

At the heart of the story, I wanted readers to understand that everyone deserves love, happiness, and acceptance.

Becoming a Published Author

The moment Between the Blue Lines became available on Amazon was an incredible feeling.

Something that had once existed only in my imagination was now a published book that people could actually read.

Was I nervous? Absolutely!

Was I excited? You bet!

Putting something you’ve created out into the world can be intimidating. You’re sharing a little piece of yourself with people you’ve never met, hoping they’ll enjoy the journey as much as you enjoyed creating it.

But becoming a published author has also opened the door to something I never expected: a wonderful community of fellow authors, readers, and people willing to share their knowledge and encouragement.

I’ve had the opportunity to meet authors who have generously offered advice, answered questions, and shared their experiences in the publishing world. Their willingness to help a new author has meant so much to me.

I’ve learned that the author community is filled with people who genuinely want to see others succeed.

And as someone who is still learning the ropes, I appreciate every bit of guidance I’ve received.

This Is Only the Beginning

One thing I’ve discovered is that once you start creating characters and building their world, they don’t necessarily leave you alone!

The Forest River Evergreens have become more than just a fictional hockey team to me. Each player has a personality, a history, and potentially a story waiting to be told.

While Between the Blue Lines introduces readers to Austin and Tristan, there are other players whose lives and relationships I’m excited to explore.

My writing journey is just getting started, and I’m looking forward to seeing where it takes me.

I never imagined that my love of reading and hockey would lead me to becoming a published author. But now that I’ve started, I’m excited to continue.

Who knows what the future holds? One thing is certain: there are more stories waiting to be written.

A Little About the Person Behind the Book

For those who don’t know me, I write under the pen name M. L. Goodlow.

Before becoming an author, I spent 25 years serving as a Washington State Trooper and 16 years in the United States Air Force. I’m also a forensic psychophysiologist.

After retiring from law enforcement in 2023, I found myself exploring different interests and discovering new opportunities.

I’m an avid reader, a huge hockey fan, and a goalie for the Seattle Kraken Para Ice Hockey Team. When I’m not reading, writing, or spending time at the ice rink, I enjoy traveling, trying new restaurants, and spending time with my service dog, Seti.

I’ve worn many hats throughout my life, but adding published author to that list has been an exciting and unexpected experience.

And I have to say, I’m enjoying this new chapter!

A Thank You to My Readers

To everyone who has purchased Between the Blue Lines, read it, shared it, left a review, or simply encouraged me along the way, thank you.

As a new author, every bit of support means more than you know.

I hope that when you read Austin and Tristan’s story, you find yourself laughing, cheering, getting emotional, and falling in love with the Forest River Evergreens just as I did while creating them.

My goal was to write a story that entertained readers while also reminding them of the importance of love, friendship, acceptance, and being true to themselves.

If there’s one thing I hope readers take away from this book, it’s that everyone deserves to be loved for who they are, and sometimes the greatest victory has nothing to do with what happens on the ice.

After all, it’s not just hockey. It’s about love, life, and everything that happens between the blue lines.

Thank you for being part of my journey. I hope you’ll stick around because this is only the beginning!
